How it works

Three steps. No account.

  1. Tell us what you’re facing

    Your class or test, how long you have, and how solid you feel. That’s it. No name or email needed to see your plan.

  2. Get a plan with real prices

    A phase-by-phase schedule, what it would cost at each tutor level, and the free campus options to try first.

  3. We hand-match you

    Pick an option and request a match. During the pilot a person on our team finds you a tutor and replies within 48 hours. If we can’t find a good fit, we’ll say so.

Who tutors

Two kinds of help, one place to ask.

Course tutors

Matched to your Ohio State class, taught by people who took it here: students who earned an A or A-, former TAs, and grad students.

Our pilot standard: we ask for proof of the course grade or TA role before the first match.

Independent specialists

For LSAT, MCAT, GRE, GMAT and other high-stakes exams, where the right coach matters more than the right campus.

Our pilot standard: a recent qualifying score or a documented coaching track record, checked before we match.

We are recruiting tutors now. We will never show invented reviews or tutor counts: what you see here is what exists.

Full transparency

This page is an experiment. Here’s the bet.

Buckeye Tutor is a student venture built for an entrepreneurial finance class. We’d rather learn that no one wants this now than after building it.

We believe Ohio State students facing an exam scramble to find a tutor through word of mouth, with no price or quality information. If shown a concrete, priced plan and offered a hand-matched tutor, at least of our first testers will ask for a match, and the ones who do will choose options averaging an hour or more.

If not, we stop building and change the idea.

What happens to your answers

  • Your answers stay in this browser on this device. This site has no server or database.
  • We collect no name, email or student ID. The plan works without them.
  • “Request a match” opens your own email app with a draft. Nothing is sent unless you press send.
  • Our test team tallies anonymous outcomes by hand, such as “asked for a match” or “said it was too expensive”.
